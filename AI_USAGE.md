# AI usage

I used AI tools throughout this project, and I want to be fully clear about what each one did. Claude (Anthropic) helped me research the problem, verify facts, and plan the project. Codex (OpenAI) helped me write code, including the Flask server, the web app, the rules engine, and the measurement view. Claude Code helped with the graphics in my demo video. I came up with the idea and the approach (a photo check that finds body points, lets the parent tap the belt, and measures in shoulder widths), made all the major design decisions, took and staged the test photos... constructed the data set, contacted the experts, tested the app on my own iPhone, and recorded the video. The rules come from published AAP and NHTSA guidance, and the numeric thresholds are my own unvalidated estimates based on geometry and logical reasoning.
